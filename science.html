The science behind Zervion

How the model works, where the data comes from, and what it cannot do yet.

How the model works

Equilibrium

A logistic equilibrium curve per reaction, shifted by pressure, limits how much reacts in one pass.

Kinetics

Arrhenius rates scaled by catalyst activity and loading give the per-pass conversion.

Plant

Recycle sets overall yield. Compression and heating set energy. Hydrogen, power, catalyst and operating cost set the price per tonne.

Carbon

Grid intensity and the hydrogen source set CO₂ per tonne of product.

Limits

Snapshot values are rounded and indicative. The model is simplified, so use it to compare options, not to design a plant. Catalysis-Hub energies are computed (DFT), not measured in a lab.
